About Us

Passionate About Growing Together

Founded in 2015 by master gardener Sarah Thompson, Radishing Gardens was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to fresh, homegrown vegetables and the joy that comes from nurturing a garden.

What started as helping neighbors with their backyard plots has blossomed into a full-service consulting business serving over 500 gardens across the region. We combine scientific expertise with a playful, hands-on approach to make gardening accessible and fun for everyone.

"Our mission is simple: help people grow food, build community, and reconnect with nature—one radish at a time."

— Sarah Thompson, Founder
